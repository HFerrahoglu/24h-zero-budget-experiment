# 24h Zero-Budget Product Experiment

An AI agent (Claude, by Anthropic) tries to find a real software problem, build a product for it, and sell it to a stranger within 24 hours, with zero budget and no existing audience. A human operator handles only the steps that require a human identity (account sign-ups, verifications, publishing posts) and follows the agent's written instructions.

Everything is logged in the open:

- `STATE.md`: current phase, next steps, open risks
- `LOG.md`: timestamped decision log (Turkish)
- `RESEARCH.md`: evidence, links, scoring
- `ACCOUNTS.md`: accounts created from scratch (no secrets)
- `OPERATOR_TASKS.md`: everything the operator was asked to do, plus time spent
- `METRICS.md`: funnel metrics per channel (no invented numbers)
- `REPORT.md`: final report

Start: 2026-11-05 16:00 (UTC+3). End: 2026-11-06 16:00 (UTC+3).

## Result

The experiment has ended. **No sale: 0 sales, $0 revenue in 24 hours.**

- **Product:** [Where Can I Post?](https://hferrahoglu.github.io/where-can-i-post/), a free finder that shows which launch channels will reject a post from a brand-new account with AI-assisted text. A $7 full kit covers 50 channels.
- **Funnel:** 6 tracked visits, 2 demo uses, 0 buy clicks, 0 sales. The agent's own test visits are excluded.
- **Was the need real? Partially proven.**
  - The problem itself was confirmed: one independent maker said the findings matched what they had hit, another called it "a very real problem", and the experiment's own new accounts hit the same walls (Reddit filter, Indie Hackers posting lock, a 4-month directory queue).
  - Willingness to pay was not measured. Six visits are too few to say whether anyone would pay.
- **Main lesson:** Distribution failed, not the build. The people this tool is for gather in the channels that are closed to new accounts and AI-assisted posts. The posts (Reddit, two Bluesky threads, an Indie Hackers product page) brought 0 tracked visits. The only real conversation, and the only channel-tracked visit, came from one useful comment on an existing thread.
- **Cost:** $0. No paid plans, ads, domains or paid APIs.
- **What happens now:** The tool stays online and the full kit stays on sale. The data is a 2026-11-05 snapshot and isn't updated on a schedule.

The full report is in [`REPORT.md`](REPORT.md) (Turkish), with per-channel numbers, the best and worst decisions, operator time and a zero-cost audit.
